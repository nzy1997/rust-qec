Run it

One command from circuit to detector samples

cargo run -p rstim --bin rstim -- detect --in circuit.stim --shots 1000 --out_format dets

Use stats to inspect a circuit or replace detect with sample for raw measurement shots.

Primary comparison: repeated sampling after compilation

Both implementations compile the same circuit and build its reference sample once before timing. The primary timer runs inside each worker and includes sampling plus the same b8 encoding, but excludes pipe transfer. The 1.55 MB result is still transferred and validated after every call, and its full end-to-end time is shown separately.

FixtureImplementationThroughputSample + b8With transfer
Surface d=11, r=100rstim precompiled steady-state77,366 shots/s13.236 ms13.570 ms
Surface d=11, r=100Stim precompiled steady-state63,645 shots/s16.089 ms16.464 ms

Each value is the median of 7 subsequent 1,024-shot calls after 2 warmups. In this recorded run, rstim has 1.22× lower sample-plus-b8 wall time; the separately recorded end-to-end result is consistent at 1.21×.
