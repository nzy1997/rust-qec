From Stim circuit to SVG or JSON

QP101 is a JSON format for circuit diagrams. It preserves gates, repeat blocks, detectors, noise and coordinates in execution order.

{% include "partials/example-circuit.html" %} 
rstim render_svg --in circuit.stim --out circuit.svg
rstim export_json --in circuit.stim --out circuit.qp101.json

circuit.svg shows the two-qubit circuit; circuit.qp101.json is its ordered circuit document.

Read the diagram

R resets a qubit; XE marks a possible X error, and the filled dot is the CNOT control. M marks a measurement. D0 and D1 label detector checks; L0 labels the logical parity. This is the circuit definition, before sampling a shot.
