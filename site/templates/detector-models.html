1. Extract the error model

{% include "partials/example-circuit.html" %}

A detector error model (DEM) lists independent error mechanisms and the detector or logical bits each can flip.

rstim circuit dem --in circuit.stim --out model.dem > /dev/null
cat model.dem
error(0.1) D0 D1
error(0.1) D1 L0

An X error on q0 flips D0 and D1; an X error on q1 flips D1 and the logical observable L0. Each mechanism has probability 0.1.

2. Sample the extracted model

rstim sample_dem --in model.dem --shots 1 --seed 86 --out_format dets
shot D0 L0

The dets format lists only labels that flipped. Here both mechanisms occurred: their two D1 flips cancel, leaving D0 and L0. DEM sampling uses its own random stream; its seed does not identify the same physical shot as circuit sampling.
