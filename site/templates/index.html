What's in the box

One workspace, the whole decoder-experiment loop

Simulate circuits, extract detector error models, decode with three decoder families, and benchmark the results — without leaving the workspace.

Simulate and sample circuits

Parse Stim-format circuits, inspect structure with rstim stats, and sample measurement or detector shots.

Save decoder and training data

Choose raw shots or aligned public/private bundles, understand b8, and preserve atom loss as a separate model input.

Extract detector error models

Derive DEMs with rstim analyze_errors and sample them directly with rstim sample_dem.

Decode with three decoder families

MWPM (rmatching), BP-OSD (rbposd), and ILP (rilpqec) decoders share one workspace and harness.

Archive decoder sample results

RSMP v1 stores every measurement result in a circuit-bound archive, then recovers measurements, detectors, and observables with the original circuit.

Render circuit diagrams

Render SVG diagrams with rstim render_svg, including seeded atom-loss sample-shot overlays, and export QP101 JSON.

Construct CSS codes

Build CSS code matrices with qec-code and run exact or randomized distance checks backed by qec-ilp-core.
